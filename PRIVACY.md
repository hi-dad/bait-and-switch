# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 31, 2026*

Bait and Switch is a Chrome extension that replaces YouTube thumbnails with real frames from inside each video.

## What the extension collects

Nothing. Bait and Switch does not collect, sell or share any personal information, and it does not use analytics, ads or an account. The developer never receives any data from it.

## What stays on your device

The extension saves one setting: the thumbnail mode you choose in its menu. It's kept in Chrome's own extension storage. If you use Chrome sync, Chrome may copy this setting to your other devices through your Google account.

Video frames are compared on your own computer, and those results are not saved or sent anywhere.

## What YouTube can see

The extension loads frame images from `i.ytimg.com`, YouTube's image server. Your browser requests these images the same way it requests any other image on YouTube, so Google can see those requests. Your use of YouTube is covered by Google's own privacy policy.
